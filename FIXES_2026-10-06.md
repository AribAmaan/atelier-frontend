## Birthdate field (mobile)
- New components/BirthdateField.tsx: Day / Month / Year selects replace <input type="date"> on signup and the Google "complete profile" step. The native date input opens at today's year on phones, can collapse on iOS Safari, and the Google form lacked dark color-scheme. The selects always produce a valid YYYY-MM-DD (day list follows month/year, so Feb 30 can't be chosen), use local time, and show an inline 18+ notice. The backend remains the authority on age.
