# RoliChat search visibility update

The homepage now serves a descriptive title and summary, a canonical URL, social sharing metadata, and WebSite structured data. Its visible hero copy names AI character chat and roleplay. The site also serves `/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml`; the sitemap lists the public homepage and legal pages.

Deploy this frontend through the existing Netlify build. After the deploy, verify that `https://rolichat.com/robots.txt` and `https://rolichat.com/sitemap.xml` return successfully, then submit the sitemap and inspect the homepage URL in Google Search Console. Search visibility and ranking depend on Google's crawl and indexing and cannot be guaranteed by these changes alone.
